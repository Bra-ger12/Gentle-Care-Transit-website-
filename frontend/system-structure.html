GENTLE CARE TRANSIT · SYSTEM MAP

How the system is structured

Based on the NEMT system design — two entry points lead to three role-based portals. Solid boxes are built screens; dashed boxes are functions inside a portal.

ENTRY · PUBLIC

Login & Registration

Sign in / create account · Rider & Driver role switch · forgot password · remember me · terms consent

ENTRY · STAFF ONLY

Admin Login

Staff email + password · invitation-only accounts (two-factor & trusted-device planned)

ACTOR · PATIENT

Patient Portal

Profile: name, address, contacts, medical notes

Request a Ride

Booking request → 0..1 trip once approved & assigned

Track Trip Status

Live status of the ongoing trip

Trip History

All completed rides

Billing

Automatic billing notifications after trip completion

Rating & Review

Rate the driver; feeds driver performance rating

ACTOR · DRIVER

Driver Portal

Profile: license, availability, rating · linked vehicle (plate, model, type) · 1..* trips

Set Availability

Online / offline status toggle

Accept Trip

Notification of assigned trip → accept → trip becomes active

View & Update Trip Status

Progress updates the patient tracks live; mark complete

Generate Reports

Trips, miles, and earnings summaries

ACTOR · ADMIN

Admin Console

Control center — oversees the entire system

Manage Accounts

Patient and driver accounts; view all data

Manage Trip Operations

Review booking requests, assign trips to available drivers

Vehicle Management

Fleet records: plate number, model, vehicle type

Generate Reports

System-wide reporting across trips, drivers, billing

Overall system flow

1 · PATIENT

Registers → logs in → books a ride → submits request

2 · ADMIN

Reviews the request → assigns trip to an available driver

3 · DRIVER

Gets notified → accepts → trip active → updates status while patient tracks

4 · COMPLETION

Trip completes → automatic billing notification → rating & review

Throughout: admin manages the system and generates reports.
