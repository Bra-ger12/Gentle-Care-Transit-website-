Terms

Terms of service

These terms cover booking and travelling with Gentle Care Transit. They are written to be read, not to be skipped.

This is not an ambulance service

Gentle Care Transit provides non-emergency medical transportation. Our drivers are not paramedics and our vehicles do not carry emergency medical equipment. If someone needs urgent medical help, call 911. Do not book a trip and wait.

Who can book

Anyone with a confirmed account can book for themselves. A caregiver or family member can book on a rider's behalf by calling dispatch. Drivers cannot book trips.

Booking and notice

  • Bookings require a minimum amount of notice, set by your provider and shown on the booking form.
  • Both ends of the trip must fall inside the service area. The booking form lists the towns covered.
  • Tell us your mobility needs and how many people are travelling with you. A van that arrives without a lift, or without enough seats, is a missed appointment.

Changes and cancellations

You can cancel or reschedule up to the cutoff shown on your trip — normally 24 hours before pickup. Inside that window, call dispatch: staff can move a trip when you cannot. Rescheduling an assigned trip releases the driver, and the trip returns to dispatch to be re-crewed.

Being ready for your ride

Please be ready at the pickup point at the scheduled time. Your driver will record their arrival and then wait — the wait is normally 10 minutes. If you have not appeared by the end of it, the driver may record a no-show and move on to their next rider, and the trip may be billed. If you are running late, call dispatch rather than letting the clock run.

Round trips and will-call returns

A round trip is booked as two separate rides, and the return may be given to a different driver. If you choose will-call because you do not know when your appointment will finish, you must tell us you are ready — press "I'm ready to go home" on your trip page or call dispatch. Nobody is dispatched until you do.

What we ask of you

  • Give accurate addresses and contact details.
  • Wear a seatbelt, and allow the driver to secure a wheelchair properly.
  • Treat drivers with courtesy. We will end service to anyone who threatens or abuses our staff.
  • Do not ask a driver to make unscheduled stops or to carry passengers not on the booking.

Fares and billing

Trips are priced from a published rate schedule: a base fare, a per-mile rate, a surcharge for wheelchair and stretcher transport, and a charge for waiting time beyond the free allowance. Where a journey is covered by Medicaid, we bill the payer directly. Where it is not, the invoice is yours.

Mileage is normally measured from a routing service. When that service is unavailable, mileage is estimated and the record is marked as an estimate. If you believe a distance is wrong, say so and we will review it.

Service limits

We schedule in good faith but cannot guarantee arrival times. Weather, road closures, and earlier trips overrunning all affect a shift — North Dakota winters especially. We will tell you as soon as we know a trip is at risk. Our liability is limited to the fare paid for the affected trip.

Accounts

Keep your password to yourself. Driver accounts stay pending until an administrator has checked the driver's licence, vehicle and insurance. We may suspend an account that is being misused.

Contact

Dispatch: (701) 555-0100. See the contact page for hours and written enquiries.

These terms describe how the service is intended to operate. They are a starting point, not legal advice, and your provider should have them reviewed against North Dakota transportation and Medicaid regulations before relying on them.
