Privacy

How we handle your information

Gentle Care Transit arranges non-emergency medical transportation. Doing that means holding information about where you live, where you are being treated, and how you travel. This page says plainly what we keep, why, and who sees it.

What we hold

  • Who you are — name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and your emergency contact.
  • Where you travel — pickup and drop-off addresses, appointment times, and the notes you leave for the driver.
  • What you need — your mobility requirements (wheelchair, stretcher, walker, oxygen) and how many people travel with you.
  • Billing details — your Medicaid member ID and prior authorisation number where the trip is reimbursed.
  • Trip records — timestamps for each stage of the ride, odometer readings, and the signature taken at drop-off.

Why we hold it

To dispatch a suitable vehicle to the right place at the right time, to bill the payer for a journey we actually made, and to answer questions afterwards about a trip that went wrong. Mobility information is not optional detail: sending a sedan to a wheelchair user is a failed trip and a missed appointment.

Who sees it

  • Your driver sees your name, phone number, addresses, mobility need and notes — for the trip they are assigned, from the point they are assigned it.
  • Dispatch staff see the same information for all trips, because they are the people who fix problems.
  • Payers receive the reimbursement claim: dates, mileage, member ID, prior authorisation and the signature reference.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising. Every time a staff member opens or edits a record, that access is written to an audit log with their name, the time, and their IP address.

Location data

While a driver is on duty, their app reports the vehicle's position so dispatch can see the fleet and send the nearest suitable van. We keep only the most recent position for each driver — it is overwritten, not appended, so the system holds no history of anyone's movements. During a live trip, that position is visible to you so you can see your ride approaching.

How long we keep it

Trip and billing records are retained for the period the payer and North Dakota record-keeping rules require. Account details are kept while your account is open. Ask us to close your account and we will, subject to those retention obligations on completed trips.

Your choices

  • Read and correct your own details from your account page.
  • Turn individual notification categories on or off from the same page.
  • Ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to correct something, by calling dispatch.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Sign-in sessions are short-lived and can be revoked. Uploaded documents are checked before they are stored. Access is limited by role: a driver cannot open a trip that is not theirs, and no query returns another rider's record.

Contact

Questions about this page, or about a specific record, go to dispatch on (701) 555-0100, or see the contact page.
