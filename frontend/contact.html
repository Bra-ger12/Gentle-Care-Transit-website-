What to have ready

Calls go faster when you have these to hand:

  • The rider's name and date of birth.
  • The appointment time, and the clinic or hospital name.
  • Pickup address, including any entrance the driver should use.
  • Mobility needs, and whether anyone is travelling with the rider.
  • For a change to an existing trip: the trip date and time.
